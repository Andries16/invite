## Goal
Text, quote, story/memory, timeline and footer sections.

## Scope
- Text: heading and paragraphs.
- Quote: text and attribution.
- Story/memory: entries with optional media.
- Timeline: ordered moments with dates.
- Footer: closing message and links generated by the platform.

## Deliverables
- `v1/sections/{text,quote,story,timeline,footer}.ts`.
