# Invite.md HTML Prototype

This is a product prototype for the complete Invite.md AI Experience Studio.

The prototype demonstrates:

- AI creative director and conversational creation
- Story intelligence and memory extraction
- Experience recipes and visual languages
- Scene/runtime composition
- RSVP, quiz, branching, guestbook, voting, photo submissions and reveals
- Media intelligence
- Invitations and versioned editing
- Campaign personalization
- Guest journey analytics
- Stable publication URLs
- QR and custom-domain distribution
- Physical and digital exports
- Localization
- Workspace collaboration and review
- Billing, usage and entitlements
- Experience recipe marketplace
- Guest playback

The prototype is standalone HTML/CSS/JS and does not represent backend implementation.

Keep its flows aligned with docs/FEATURES.md, docs/ROADMAP.md and tasks/.
